    Intermittent fasting

    Set your eating window for each weekday. The header is green while you may eat and red while you fast. Outside the window counts as fasting, and a window over midnight (e.g. 20:00–02:00) works too. When you open the app you are asked how yesterday went.

    Calorie tracking beta

    Log food for breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks. Product and barcode search uses the free Open Food Facts database; the text you search for is sent to openfoodfacts.org. Everything you log stays on this device.

    Backup

    Your history only lives on this device. Export it now and then.
